Automation · Part 4

Auto Wheelspins

Run Super or normal Wheelspins, collect prizes, and handle duplicate-car prompts automatically.

1

Templates are built in

The Auto Wheelspins feature is detection-based, and the templates it needs ship built in — nothing to capture, works out of the box.

2

Pick the wheel type and go

Choose Super Wheelspin (3 prizes at once) or normal Wheelspin (1 prize). In-game, open the My Horizon menu so the matching tile is visible. If you captured the My Horizon tab template, you can also start from the main menu — FAFE clicks the tab for you.

Super Wheelspin is on the left, normal Wheelspin on the right. Use the tile matching your FAFE setting.
3

Set keep conditions

Duplicate cars are now sold automatically. Check "Keep Forza Edition (FE) cars" and/or set a "Keep if value ≥" credit threshold (0 = sell all) to hold onto the ones you want. ⚠ Double-check your threshold so you don't sell a car you meant to keep.

4

Pick wheel type, set the count and start

Use the buttons to choose Super (3 prizes) or Normal (1 prize), and set the number of spins (0 = unlimited, stop with F9). Open Auto Wheelspins in the sidebar, then click ▶ Start or press F9. The countdown is 3 seconds; with background mode enabled, you don't need to switch back to Forza.

Steps 3–4: spin count, wheel type, and duplicate-car keep conditions.
5

Fully automatic

FAFE clicks the chosen Wheelspin tile to start, then every spin auto-continues: detect the prompt → (optional) skip → Enter collects the prize (all 3 at once for a Super Wheelspin) → handle 0–3 duplicate cars. FAFE never presses to spin itself.

6

Stop

Press F9 or click ■ Stop anytime. With a set count, the final spin collects with Esc and ends back on the My Horizon menu.
